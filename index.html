◎Analysis Context

01 / Liquidity

Observe equal highs/lows, sweeps and whether price reclaims a level.

02 / Structure

Check swing highs/lows, BOS and CHOCH. Avoid forcing a direction in a range.

03 / Imbalance

Only mark FVG or imbalance areas when they are actually visible in the chart.

04 / Confirmation

A single candle is not certainty. Consider location, structure and momentum together.

05 / No Trade

Conflicting evidence, blurry charts or nearby opposing levels can justify waiting.
