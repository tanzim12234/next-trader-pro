GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Does it place trades automatically?

No. It is a manual-first analysis and tracking workspace. It does not submit broker orders or control your Quotex account.

Are all feeds guaranteed real-time?

No. Availability and delay depend on the instrument and provider. Compare the displayed source and price with your broker.

Does a CALL or PUT guarantee a win?

No. Markets are uncertain, data can differ between providers, and short-expiry trading carries substantial risk.

Can I use it on mobile?

The interface is responsive. Browser screen sharing may work best on desktop.

Where are membership options?

Create an account or sign in, then open the membership page for current subscription details.
